ABOUT MERIDIAN

Intentional Bodywork

Meridian exists to help people reconnect with their bodies through therapeutic touch, nervous system regulation, recovery practices, and restorative care.

The Meridian Philosophy

Every session is customized to support the unique needs of the person on the table. Rather than applying a one-size-fits-all approach, Meridian draws from a growing library of therapeutic modalities to create meaningful results.


From stress and burnout to athletic recovery and nervous system restoration, every treatment is designed with intention.
